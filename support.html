GhostKey Support

Help, privacy, and troubleshooting for the GhostKey Safari extension.

Canonical support page

The primary GhostKey support page is published at https://tarunag10.github.io/GhostKey/support.html.

How do I enable GhostKey?

After installation, enable GhostKey in Safari Settings or Safari Extensions preferences.

How do I pause GhostKey on a site?

Use the extension popup to pause GhostKey on the current site or manage trusted sites.

What if GhostKey hides something it should not?

Use undo, pause GhostKey on that site, or add the site to your allowlist.
