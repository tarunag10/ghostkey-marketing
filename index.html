How it works

GhostKey identifies high-confidence sign-in prompts, hides the matching overlay elements, and restores page usability when scrolling or interaction has been blocked.

1

Detect

GhostKey looks for sign-in overlays that interrupt reading.

2

Dismiss

Matching prompts are hidden locally in Safari.

3

Restore

Page scrolling and interaction are restored when blockers are removed.

Local by design

GhostKey works in your browser. It does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not send page content to developer servers.

  • No tracking
  • No analytics
  • No remote processing
