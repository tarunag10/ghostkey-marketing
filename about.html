About GhostKey

GhostKey is a Safari extension that helps reduce intrusive sign-in prompts and restore page usability.

Purpose

GhostKey helps reduce interruptions from sign-in prompts that cover a page, lock scrolling, blur content, or block normal page interaction.

Controls

Users can pause GhostKey per site, add sites to an allowlist, disable GhostKey globally, and undo the most recent page change.

Safety boundaries

GhostKey avoids checkout, payment, upload, navigation, and cookie consent flows. Behavior may vary by page or site.
