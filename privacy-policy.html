Privacy Policy

GhostKey does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not send page content to developer servers.

Primary privacy policy

The canonical GhostKey privacy policy is published at https://tarunag10.github.io/GhostKey/privacy-policy.html.

Summary

GhostKey stores extension settings locally in browser storage. These settings include enabled state, aggressive mode, debug mode, disabled sites, and allowlisted hosts. These settings stay on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer.

User controls

Users can disable GhostKey on individual sites, add sites to the allowlist, turn off GhostKey globally, and undo the last suppression action.
